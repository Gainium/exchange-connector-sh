# Plan 015 — Kraken spot balance reports held funds as free

Spec: `specs/015.kraken-spot-balance-reports-held-funds-as-free.md`.

## Solution
The split is a pure function, `krakenSpotFreeLocked` in
`src/exchange/exchanges/kraken/balance.ts`. Spot `getBalance` swaps
`getAccountBalance()` for `getExtendedBalance()` and spreads the helper's result.
Limiter key, error handling and retry path are untouched.

## Blast Radius
1. Every consumer of `/balance` for Kraken spot now sees a smaller `free` wherever
   orders are resting. That is the correction, but it is a visible change: a bot
   that sized against the inflated figure will now be told the truth.
2. **Must ship with `main-app-sh` spec 069.** The Kraken spot v2 balance stream
   publishes the wallet TOTAL as `free` with no `locked`. Once REST writes a real
   hold, a consumer that stores that total verbatim beside it double-counts the
   hold until the next REST refresh.
3. `verify` and the fee-volume lookup keep using the basic `Balance` endpoint —
   they only test reachability / read a total.
4. Rate cost: `BalanceEx` is metered the same as `Balance`.
