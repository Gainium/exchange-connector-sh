# Plan 034 — OKX order re-submitted under its own clOrdId

Spec: `specs/034.okx-order-resubmitted-under-its-own-clordid.md`.

## Build order
1. `src/exchange/exchanges/okx/clordid-resubmit.spec.ts` with stubbed REST
   clients (same harness shape as `xperp-instid.spec.ts`). Red on HEAD.
2. `okx/index.ts`:
   - `openOrder`: split the submit from the read-back. A read-back failure
     goes to `handleOkxErrors(this.getOrder, …)`. A submit failure goes to
     `handleOkxErrors(this.resubmitOrder, …)`. A `51016` is resolved through
     `findOrderByClientId` first.
   - `resubmitOrder`: look up first, then `openOrder`.
   - `findOrderByClientId`: one `getOrderDetails` by clOrdId. `undefined` on a
     miss or on an error. It never throws.
   - The read-back's `getOrder` fallback now gets the Gainium pair, not the
     instId. `getOrder` maps the symbol itself, and `updateSymbol` is not
     idempotent on SWAP (`X-SWAP` → `X-SWAP-SWAP`).

## Blast radius
- Only `OKXExchange.openOrder` and its retry. The `BaseReturn<CommonOrder>`
  shape is unchanged, so main-app consumers see an `OK` order where they
  used to see a `NOTOK` "already exists".
- The request body is unchanged, and no new submit path exists. The only extra
  venue traffic is one `getOrderDetails` before a re-submit or after a
  `51016`.
