# 006 — Bitget futures balance: the position margin is counted twice

Bug #594 ("Incorrect Portfolio Balance calculation for Bitget USDT-M
Futures"). Symptom: the Portfolio Balances widget shows a Bitget USDT-M
connection whose **Total** is far larger than the wallet balance the exchange
itself reports, because "Not in limit" already *is* the whole wallet balance
and "In limit" is then added on top of it.

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 — expected

`BitgetExchange.futures_getBalance()` returns, per margin coin, a
`{ free, locked }` pair whose **sum is the coin's wallet balance on the
venue** — the same invariant every other futures connector here already
honours:

| venue | `free` | `locked` | `free + locked` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bybit (`bybit/index.ts` `getBalance`) | `walletBalance - locked` | `locked + totalOrderIM + totalPositionIM` | `walletBalance` |
| Binance (`binance/index.ts` `futures_getBalance`) | `maxWithdrawAmount` | `walletBalance - maxWithdrawAmount` | `walletBalance` |
| KuCoin (`kucoin/index.ts` `futures_getBalance`) | `availableBalance` | `positionMargin + orderMargin + frozenFunds` | account equity |

The invariant is load-bearing rather than cosmetic: every consumer of
`FreeAsset` treats the two fields as a partition of one balance and renders
or reasons about `free + locked` as the total (main-app
`core/src/graphql/handlers/balance.handler.ts` → `const total = d.free +
d.locked`; the dashboard's Portfolio Balances widget labels the same two
fields "NOT IN LIMIT" / "IN LIMIT" and sums them into "TOTAL").

### 1.2 — actual

```ts
const available = +d.available
const margin = +(d as any).isolatedMargin + +(d as any).crossedMargin
res.push({ asset: d.marginCoin, free: available, locked: margin })
```

Bitget's `available` is **not** net of the margin backing an open **crossed**
position — the funds keep counting as available because in cross mode the
whole balance backs the position. So `free` comes back as the entire wallet
balance while `locked` adds the position margin on top of it, and
`free + locked` overstates the account by exactly the crossed margin.

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 — the venue's own figures, read at the same moment as ours

For one USDT-M connection holding three **cross-margin** positions, the
exchange account panel and our stored balance were read within a minute of
each other:

| | figure |
|---|---|
| venue: USDT **wallet balance** | `W` |
| venue: **available** | `0.11 × W` |
| venue: unrealized PnL | `-0.033 × W` |
| venue: margin used by the three crossed positions | `0.76 × W` |
| ours: `free` ("Not in limit") | `1.00 × W` |
| ours: `locked` ("In limit") | `0.76 × W` |
| ours: `free + locked` ("Total") | **`1.76 × W`** |

Two independent facts fall out of that table:

- our `free` tracks the **wallet balance**, not the venue's *available*
  figure (which is ~9× smaller) — so `available` is not the spendable
  balance it is being read as;
- our `locked` tracks the **sum of the crossed positions' margin**.

### 2.2 — the same account 15 minutes later, read from production

A second read of the same connection over the dashboard GraphQL
(`getBalances`) returned `locked` lower by exactly the margin of the one
position that had been closed in between, and `free` still equal to the
wallet balance. The mapping in §1.2 therefore ran, produced these numbers,
and is stable — this is not a one-off snapshot artefact.

### 2.3 — an idle account, from the venue's own published example

Bitget's documented sample body for an account with no positions reads
`available == accountEquity == crossedMaxAvailable == maxTransferOut`. With
nothing open, `available` *is* the equity; it does not become a
"spendable" figure until positions exist, and even then (§2.1) it does not
net crossed margin out.

### 2.4 — how the two terms are documented

- `accountEquity` — "Account equity (margin coin), **including unrealized
  PnL** (based on mark price)".
- `unrealizedPL` — unrealized PnL.

so `accountEquity - unrealizedPL` is the coin's wallet balance, which in
§2.1 reproduces `W` exactly (`0.967 W - (-0.033 W) = W`). `crossedMaxAvailable`
/ `isolatedMaxAvailable` are the per-mode spendable figures; `available` is
neither.

### 2.5 — history

`v1.6.1` changed this mapping from `free = available - locked`,
`locked = locked + isolatedMargin` to the current form, which both dropped
the venue's own order-frozen `locked` term and started adding `crossedMargin`
to a `free` that already contained it.

## 3. Assumption (hypothesis)

The root cause is the **anchor field**: `available` is being used as though
it were "wallet balance minus everything committed", and it is not. Any
mapping that starts from `available` and then *adds* a margin term will
double-count in at least one margin mode, because whether `available` nets a
given margin type out is mode-dependent and undocumented.

Anchoring on a figure that is defined for the whole account —
`accountEquity - unrealizedPL`, the wallet balance — and then *splitting* it
into `locked` (margin + frozen) and `free` (the remainder) removes the
question entirely: the total is right in both margin modes by construction,
and only the split between the two fields depends on the margin terms.

Consequence if true: for a flat account (no positions, no PnL) the mapping is
unchanged — `accountEquity == available` (§2.3), `locked == 0` — so only
accounts with open positions move.
