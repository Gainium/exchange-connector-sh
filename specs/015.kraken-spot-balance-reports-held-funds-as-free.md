# 015 — Kraken spot reports funds held by open orders as free

Bug spec. Plan: `plans/015.kraken-spot-balance-reports-held-funds-as-free.md`.
Pairs with `main-app-sh` spec 069 (a stream total must not be stored as `free`).

## §1 Definition
- §1.1 Expected: `GET /balance` for a Kraken spot account splits each asset the
  way every other venue does — `locked` is what open orders hold, `free` is what
  can still be traded, and `free + locked` is the wallet total.
- §1.2 Actual: every asset comes back with `locked: 0` and `free` = the wallet
  total. Funds committed to resting limit orders — placed by a bot or by hand on
  the venue — read as spendable everywhere downstream.

## §2 Evidence
- `src/exchange/exchanges/kraken/index.ts` `getBalance` (spot branch) calls the
  basic `Balance` endpoint, which returns one number per asset, and hardcodes
  `locked: 0`.
- Kraken's `BalanceEx` returns `{balance, credit, credit_used, hold_trade}` per
  asset and documents tradable funds as
  `balance + credit - credit_used - hold_trade`
  (<https://docs.kraken.com/api/docs/rest-api/get-extended-balance>). It needs the
  same "Query funds" permission as `Balance`.

## §3 Assumption
The hold is absent because the endpoint in use cannot express it, not because it
is dropped later. `spot-balance-hold.spec.ts` stubs both endpoints and fails on a
connector that reads the basic one.

## §4 Required behaviour
- §4.1 Spot `getBalance` reads `BalanceEx`; `locked` = `hold_trade`, `free` =
  Kraken's tradable formula.
- §4.2 A credit line counts toward `free` exactly as Kraken's formula states.
- §4.3 Neither figure is ever negative, `NaN`, or carries binary subtraction
  residue; a missing field reads as 0.
- §4.4 The futures branch and the `BaseReturn<FreeAsset>` shape are unchanged.
