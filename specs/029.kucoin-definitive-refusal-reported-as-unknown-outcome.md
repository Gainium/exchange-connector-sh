# 029 — A KuCoin order refused on every attempt was reported as an unknown outcome

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

- **§1.1.1** When every attempt of a KuCoin call ends in a definitive answer from
  the venue (for example `200004` Balance insufficient), the reason returned
  once the retries are spent is the venue's own, without the
  `exchangeProblems` transport prefix (`Exchange connector | `).
- **§1.1.2** When at least one attempt failed in a way that may have reached the
  venue (a timeout, a network failure, a 5xx), the prefix is kept, even if
  later attempts were definitive refusals. A later "insufficient" can be the
  consequence of an earlier attempt that did land.
- **§1.1.3** Which codes are retried, and how often, does not change.

### §1.2 Actual (before)

`handleKucoinErrors` prefixed every exhausted-retry reason with
`exchangeProblems`. Callers read that prefix as "the venue's state is unknown",
so a plain funds refusal was re-resolved and re-sent by the caller, and the
caller's record of the order was kept open as if it might be resting.
