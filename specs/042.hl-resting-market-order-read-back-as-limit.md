# 042 — a Hyperliquid MARKET order that rests is read back as a LIMIT at the request price

Bug spec (SKILL.md Part 0). Scope: `src/exchange/exchanges/hyperliquid/index.ts`
(`openOrder`, and the `convertOrder` comment).

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

`openOrder` for a requested MARKET order returns the order as `type: 'MARKET'`,
and its `price` is the price the order really has on the venue: the fill price
when it filled, otherwise the (padded) limit price it rests at.

### §1.2 Actual

Hyperliquid has no market order type. `openOrder` sends a MARKET as a GTC limit
padded through the book (`price * 1.1` buy, `price * 0.9` sell). When the place
response is `resting` rather than `filled`, `openOrder` read the order back with
`filledPrice = order.price` — the un-padded REQUESTED price — and `convertOrder`
reports HL's `orderType: 'Limit'`. The result is `type: 'LIMIT'` at exactly the
requested price with nothing executed, which the consumer classifies as a
resting LIMIT order (requested price = reply price, no fills, no quote).

## §2 Evidence

- Production: a combo base order built as MARKET at 0.050678 (spot SELL) was
  stored as `type LIMIT, price 0.04561, origPrice 0.050678`; the venue ack was
  `NEW` and the fill came 4 s later at 0.04561 — the padded price.
- Reproduced through the real `openOrder` with fake venue clients
  (`open-order-market-resting.spec.ts`): before the change a resting MARKET SELL
  at 0.050678 comes back `LIMIT 0.050678` while the fake venue holds 0.045610.
- The consumer's own classifier (`placedOrderType`, main-app core) returns LIMIT
  for that reply and MARKET for the corrected one.
- Origin: latent — the `${order.price}` fallback dates from the Hyperliquid
  integration (1ab4275, 2025-09-24); the padding was added later (2e2ff40,
  2025-11-03) without updating it.

## §3 Assumption (root cause, hypothesis)

The read-back is seeded with the requested price and the venue's order type,
neither of which describes a MARKET we sent as a padded limit. Seeding no price
(so `convertOrder` falls back to `limitPx`, the price HL holds) and reporting the
type we placed fixes it.

## §4 Requirements

- §4.1 Requested MARKET, place response `resting`, still open on read-back →
  `type: 'MARKET'`, `price` = the padded price resting on the venue.
- §4.2 Requested MARKET, place response `filled` → `type: 'MARKET'`, `price` =
  the fill price.
- §4.3 Requested LIMIT → unchanged: `type: 'LIMIT'` at the requested price.
- §4.4 The price SENT is unchanged (MARKET sell `* 0.9`, buy `* 1.1`, GTC).

## §5 Known limit

`getOrder` called on its own (status polls) still reports HL's `Limit` type for
a MARKET order: HL does not keep what we asked for. main-app keeps the stored
order's type for Hyperliquid, so the placement read-back is what decides it.
