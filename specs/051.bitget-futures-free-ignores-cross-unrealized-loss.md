# 051 — Bitget futures `free` ignores the open cross positions' unrealized loss

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 — expected

On the linear product types (USDT-FUTURES / USDC-FUTURES),
`BitgetExchange.futures_getBalance()` reports as `free` no more than the venue
itself will let the account commit to a new order: `crossedMaxAvailable`.
In cross margin mode that figure nets the unrealized loss of every open cross
position out of the balance.

### 1.2 — the partition is kept

`free + locked` stays the coin's wallet balance (spec 006 §1.1). Whatever the
cap takes off `free` moves to `locked`. That is the same convention Binance
uses, where `free = maxWithdrawAmount` (net of open losses) and
`locked = walletBalance - maxWithdrawAmount`.

### 1.3 — never raises `free`

The cap only ever lowers `free`. When the venue figure is above
`walletBalance - committed` (open profit, which cross mode may spend), `free`
keeps spec 006's value.

### 1.4 — missing figure

If `crossedMaxAvailable` is absent or not a number, `free` keeps spec 006's
`walletBalance - committed`. A negative figure clamps `free` to 0.

### 1.5 — actual (before)

`free = walletBalance - (locked + isolatedMargin + crossedMargin)`. With cross
positions in loss, the venue's spendable figure is lower by the size of the
loss, so a DCA bot saw enough free balance for its next safety order and sent
it. The venue refused it ("the order amount exceeds the balance"), and the
bot's "Not enough balance" message then quoted a free balance several times
larger than the order.

## 2. Evidence

- **Arithmetic on a live account (read-only).** A USDT-M cross account had
  two 1x longs. `crossedMargin` matched the positions' notional at mark price
  to within a cent, so this mapping produced the stored row. The open
  positions' unrealized PnL (from entry and mark price) was large and
  negative. The figure the venue lets that account spend is therefore its
  equity less margin, which is our `free` minus that loss.
- **Refusals.** The venue refused the safety orders with "the order amount
  exceeds the balance". At each refusal our message showed `free` two or
  more times the required amount.
- **Venue fields.** `crossedMaxAvailable` is documented as the maximum
  available under cross margin mode (Bitget v2
  `/api/v2/mix/account/accounts`). On an idle account it equals `available`
  and `accountEquity` (spec 006 §2.3), so the cap changes nothing for flat
  accounts.

## 3. Assumption (hypothesis)

The venue gates a new cross order on `crossedMaxAvailable`. Our `free` is
`walletBalance - committed`, which leaves the open loss out, so it sits above
that gate by roughly the cross unrealized loss. Capping `free` at the venue's
own figure removes the gap without changing the total.

Cross is the looser mode: it may also spend open profit that isolated may
not. So the cross figure is not stricter than an isolated order would face.

Out of scope: COIN-FUTURES keeps spec 011's equity anchor. There is no
evidence on the inverse side, and its PnL denomination differs.

## Invariants

The owning invariant lives in main-app
`specs/invariants/balances.invariants.json`:
`balances.bitget-usdm-not-enough-balance-but-free-covers-it`. It flags a
Bitget USDT-M "Not enough balance" bot event whose quoted free balance is at
least the required amount, which is the message contradicting itself.
