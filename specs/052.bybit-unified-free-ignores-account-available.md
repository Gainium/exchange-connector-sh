# 052 — Bybit unified account `free` ignores the account's own available balance

## 1. Definition

### 1.1 — expected

On a Bybit Unified Trading Account, `BybitExchange.getBalance()` reports as
`free` for a margin coin of the linear contracts (USDT, USDC) no more than the
account-level `totalAvailableBalance`, converted from USD into the coin. That
is the figure the venue checks a new order's initial margin against. It nets
out the unrealized loss of open positions and every position and order margin,
whether or not the per-coin margin fields are filled in.

### 1.2 — the partition is kept

`free + locked` stays the coin's wallet balance. Whatever the cap takes off
`free` moves to `locked`, as in spec 051.

### 1.3 — never raises `free`

The cap only lowers `free`. When `totalAvailableBalance` is above
`walletBalance - locked - totalOrderIM - totalPositionIM`, `free` is unchanged.

### 1.4 — missing figure, other coins, other accounts

`free` is unchanged when `totalAvailableBalance` is absent or not a number,
for any coin other than USDT and USDC, and for any non-`UNIFIED` account
(classic `SPOT` / `CONTRACT`). A negative figure clamps `free` to 0. The USD
price of the coin is `usdValue / equity`, or 1 when either is missing.

### 1.5 — actual (before)

`free = walletBalance - (locked + totalOrderIM + totalPositionIM)`. Wallet
balance does not include unrealized PnL, so a losing account kept a `free`
figure above the venue's. On accounts whose per-coin margin fields come back
empty, `free` was the whole wallet balance. The bot sent orders the venue
refused ("ab not enough for new order"), and the "Not enough balance" message
quoted a free balance many times the required amount.

## 2. Evidence

- **Prod refusals.** Bybit linear bots in cross mode, on both the buy and the
  sell side, received "Not enough balance" refusals whose quoted `free` was
  well above `required`. Since the message started carrying the venue's reply,
  each such reply on this venue has been `ab not enough for new order`
  (available balance).
- **One account, arithmetic.** A unified account's stored USDT row read
  `free` = wallet balance and `locked` = 0 while it held a few dozen open
  linear positions at a large unrealized loss. The per-coin margin fields
  therefore came back empty, and no figure in the per-coin mapping can stand
  for the venue's available balance there.
- **Venue fields.** `totalAvailableBalance` is documented for Get Wallet
  Balance (V5) and the `wallet` stream topic as the account's available
  balance in USD (`totalMarginBalance - totalInitialMargin`), with
  `totalMarginBalance` including unrealized PnL.
- **Shared rows.** A Bybit linear leg is `linkedTo` its spot leg and reads its
  rows (main-app `sharedWallet.ts`), so the cap applies to the UNIFIED list
  whatever leg asked.

## 3. Assumption (hypothesis)

The venue accepts a new order on a unified account only while its initial
margin fits in `totalAvailableBalance`. Capping the stable margin coins at it
removes the gap without changing the total.

**Unverified — spot leg.** The cap also reaches spot buys paid in USDT/USDC on
the same unified wallet (the spot and linear legs share one row list). Bybit's
docs do not say that a spot order is gated by `totalAvailableBalance`: the
wallet-balance page defines it as the cross/portfolio *derivatives* available
balance and points spot available balance to Get Borrow Quota (Spot), whose
`spotMaxTradeAmount` is "the actual amount of quote coin you can trade or you
have", with no statement on whether derivatives loss reduces it
(https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/account/wallet-balance,
https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/order/spot-borrow-quota). If Bybit
does not net derivatives loss out of spot, this cap can block a spot buy the
venue would accept while the account's derivatives are underwater.

Out of scope: classic accounts and inverse (coin-margined) legs, which keep a
separate wallet; non-stable collateral coins (capping them could block a spot
sell the venue would accept).
