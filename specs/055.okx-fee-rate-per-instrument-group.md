# 055 — OKX fee rate is read per instrument fee group

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Test:
`src/exchange/exchanges/okx/fee-group.spec.ts`.

## §1 Definition

`GET /fees` (`getUserFees` / `getAllUserFees`) returns the account's maker/taker rate per pair. main-app
stores it and uses it as the ESTIMATE for any filled order whose observed fee it cannot book, which on OKX
includes every fill OKX reports with a fee of `0` (a zero-fee maker fill).

**Expected.** Each OKX pair carries the rate of the fee group that pair belongs to.

**Actual.** Every pair carries the same rate: the deprecated top-level `maker`/`taker` of
`/api/v5/account/trade-fee`, which OKX defines as the rate of the USDT spot pairs only. A pair in another
group (EUR, USDC, regional fiat, zero-fee, ...) is estimated at the wrong rate, so a zero-fee maker fill is
booked as a cost at the USDT-pair rate, and the deal's commission and profit are wrong.

- §1.1 The rate of a pair is the `feeGroup` entry whose `groupId` equals the instrument's `groupId`
  (from `/api/v5/public/instruments`, keyed by the Gainium pair: `instId` for spot, `instFamily` for
  futures). OKX states rates as negative = commission, so the reported rate is the value negated, as
  before.
- §1.2 A pair whose group is unknown, or whose group is absent from `feeGroup`, keeps the previous
  answer (the top-level `maker`/`taker`) — never a zero it was not given.
- §1.3 A response with no `feeGroup` at all is answered exactly as before.

## §2 Evidence

On an OKX spot account trading EUR pairs, the venue reported per-order fees of exactly 0 on maker fills
and 0.03% on taker fills, while the stored rate for every pair of that account was 0.18% / 0.30%. A closed
deal of that account booked its zero-fee maker safety orders at 0.18% each. OKX's changelog (2025-11-25):
"Get fee rates: Add new response parameter feeGroup. Fields that will be deprecated: maker, makerU,
makerUSDC, taker, takerU, takerUSDC"; instruments gained `groupId`. The public instruments feed puts USDT
spot pairs and EUR spot pairs in different groups. The adapter reads only `fees[0].maker/taker`
(`okx/index.ts` `getAllUserFees`).

## §3 Assumption

The wrong rate is the adapter reading OKX's deprecated top-level field and applying it to every pair, not
a wrong account or a caching fault: the stored rate equals one rate applied to every pair of the account.

## §4 Contract

No shape change: `/fees` still returns `{pair, maker, taker}` per pair. Only the values for pairs outside
the USDT group change.
