# 058 — Binance spot publishes its account fills

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption). Test:
`src/exchange/exchanges/binance/account-fills.spec.ts` (`spec 058` block). Extends 057, whose §1.1 scoped
spot out ("on spot, it answers an empty OK page"). That clause is superseded for spot by §1.1 below; all
other 057 clauses are unchanged.

## §1 Definition

**Expected.** On Binance spot, `getAccountFills(sinceMs?, symbol?)` returns the account's own trades on the
symbol asked for, newest first, attributed the same way as futures (057 §1.5). A caller can then tell from
the venue's own record whether a spot order the bot lost track of actually filled.

**Actual.** Spot answers an empty page. When a spot take-profit fills but the bot misses the fill (for
example, the fill event and the order-status reply are both lost), the bot keeps the deal open with a base
quantity the venue no longer holds, and no caller can read the trade that closed it.

- §1.1 With no symbol, spot answers an empty OK page and sends no request (same as futures).
- §1.2 With a symbol, spot reads `GET /api/v3/myTrades` (`getAccountTradeList`), `limit` 1000. Each page
  goes through `checkLimits('getAccountFills', 'request', 20)`, the venue's weight.
- §1.3 The venue caps a spot `myTrades` window at 24 h. `sinceMs` therefore reads seven 24 h slices walking
  back from it: slice k has `endTime = sinceMs − 1 − k·24h`, `startTime = endTime − 24h + 1`. This covers the
  same 7 days as futures from one call. With no `sinceMs` it sends one request and the venue answers its
  default (the most recent trades).
- §1.4 A spot trade has `isBuyer` instead of `side`: `isBuyer` → BUY, otherwise SELL. The fee is reported
  when its asset is the pair's quote coin (spot behaves like USDⓈ-M here); a base-coin or BNB fee is not.
- §1.5 Attribution is 057 §1.5 through the spot `getOrder` (weight 4 under the new-limit model, else 2).
  Full pages are followed forward per slice as in 057 §1.4.
- §1.6 A venue error answers NOTOK via `handleBinanceErrors`, never throws.

## §2 Evidence

A spot DCA deal stayed open holding base the account no longer had: the venue balance of the base coin was 0,
the take-profit order's client id was gone from the bot's books, and the deal could not be settled from
venue fills because the only read that could prove the fill returned an empty page on spot.

## §3 Assumption

- Read-only: no order, balance or position is changed by this route.
- No automated main-app caller reads spot account fills today (every engine consumer is gated on futures,
  Kraken or liquidation paths), so enabling spot changes no engine behaviour by itself; it gives the owner
  lookup and a future spot reconciliation path something real to read.
- 7 slices × weight 20 = 140 weight per full read, well inside the 6000/min spot budget for an
  on-demand read.
